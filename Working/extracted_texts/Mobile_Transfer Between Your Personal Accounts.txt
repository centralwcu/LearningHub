# Transfer Between Your Personal Accounts on the Mobile AppThis guide will show you how to transfer funds between your checking and saving accounts, and schedule recurring transfers1\. **Tap "Move Money"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-03/b06fbf47-a151-498b-ab97-5b7f8fe643d7/user_cropped_screenshot_ef5596ad8c9c45f996b35884c60a14af_text_export.jpeg)2\. **Tap "Internal Transfers and Payments"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-03/d62649cf-f439-4d11-af38-412a0102e09f/screenshot_bd6c4b27b3b640d8ac63dbd5f10653bf_text_export.jpeg)3\. **Tap "Select a source account" to pick the account to transfer from**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-18/f7b8060f-380e-4d3e-89aa-2a561fbee0cc/user_cropped_screenshot_5ac2847a63d342a889fb7b9b6881b9f7_text_export.jpeg)4\. **Select the account from the dropdown**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-18/96690fbf-42b5-42c1-97c8-461d82be4220/user_cropped_screenshot_ddead5da9b0c4cc9a09cc259713302d6_text_export.jpeg)5\. **Tap "Select a destination account" to select the account to transfer into**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-18/7b272156-4cd8-418a-8d90-62369f364a38/user_cropped_screenshot_59c7c61b970c4548b90a10bdb12244de_text_export.jpeg)6\. **Select an account from the dropdown**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-18/cc33950c-8041-4891-8c01-e0edfdd1b392/user_cropped_screenshot_2541177e53f14d94ad77b4cf2fffddae_text_export.jpeg)7\. **Tap the "Enter amount" field to enter an amount to transfer. The transfer date can be changed but will always default to today's date**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-18/8791e282-ecff-465c-a822-4ed74f9e37a2/user_cropped_screenshot_fcaf2a97f4b24de68e8f7b1f039106b4_text_export.jpeg)Tip: **Check the box next to "Repeat transfer" to set up the transfer to be recurring**8\. **In the "Repeat transfer" menu you can select the frequency and the end date of the recurring transfer**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-18/230f1635-8300-4872-a25d-5dbbe045da62/user_cropped_screenshot_cfb04cd021d3489b8f6d303767ad33a3_text_export.jpeg)9\. **These are the frequencies available**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-18/b4b210a7-2c6d-48f0-980a-9d76c29bf372/user_cropped_screenshot_68bf0e70d43f4bb58150fd126901b7da_text_export.jpeg)10\. **The transfer can either end on a set date or until you cancel**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-18/d3c2a414-a623-402a-97d9-972887a30d42/screenshot_a7d3468f67714891a96b5aaed112e6e3_text_export.jpeg)11\. **Tap "Transfer Funds"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-18/3a6af0c7-e91b-403b-972d-d55301a200ae/user_cropped_screenshot_085b880b734846a79d77748b80d37646_text_export.jpeg)12\. **Review the transfer information and tap "Confirm Transfer". Tapping "Edit" will let you adjust the transfer information**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-09/bca43a3a-11ed-4873-9235-0f9d383ea439/user_cropped_screenshot_5559e53e16e143dd81cced0271875753_text_export.jpeg)13\. **Tap "Done"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-18/8fe65e8f-1016-440a-8815-1829d6ffacfd/user_cropped_screenshot_209f81fe2a86411cb92498173f6e2786_text_export.jpeg)Tip: **Tapping 'Need Help" will bring you to the page to submit a support ticket. Use this for any questions you have on the transfer**
