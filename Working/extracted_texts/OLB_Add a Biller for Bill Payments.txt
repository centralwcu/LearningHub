"External Transfers & "Add Biller"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-09/b17d0034-f676-416b-a06c-9dea6ab4da86/user_cropped_screenshot_d32be9d9f009465589f01575b57aba4e_text_export.jpeg)5\. your Account Number for the
