# Making a Mobile DepositThis guide is designed to help you make a mobile deposit on the CW Banking app, and also show how to view mobile deposit history.Tip: **Deposits made after 3 PM PST, on weekends or holidays will be processed the following business day.**1\. **Tap "Deposit"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-10/9dcc179a-e434-4ace-9a85-203232c8e077/user_cropped_screenshot_fd117e43f48c4c9aaa4bf200f21ba856_text_export.jpeg)2\. **Select the account for the deposit**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-09/2fc2de5d-472d-4528-bf0d-ddd243b0b969/user_cropped_screenshot_2b5a7162a82043f784538e7493894ca3_text_export.jpeg)3\. **Enter the check amount**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-09/f7c64ee6-1a5b-4078-9c35-23b79efaa935/user_cropped_screenshot_ad5cb1ac29e24da3acec8fbc0763e4d9_text_export.jpeg)Alert: **Make sure your check is properly endorsed or it may be rejected. The endorsement needs to read "FOR MOBILE DEPOSIT ONLY AT CWCU" below your signature.**4\. **Take a picture of the front and back of your check**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-10/eeef696d-5e9f-4a80-9c1e-771a4470d4b4/File_f0cb4894d93b42179b811071176c4e98_text_export.jpeg)5\. **Check the box to accept the terms and conditions**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-09/aed3ab31-851f-44f9-a4c9-224a1f840d14/user_cropped_screenshot_364bd6d48664423184a55acd1e654cce_text_export.jpeg)6\. **Tap the i next to "Holds may apply" for deposit processing info.**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-09/3e3dc770-6427-40e4-8739-ae271645bd73/user_cropped_screenshot_184aef6cbb0246d4929045ccae7d80fc_text_export.jpeg)7\. **Tap "Deposit" to finish**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-09/9b0eec47-0839-49ad-b263-6c40a06673e7/user_cropped_screenshot_8a0dce7b3d9f46cabcf050a5b47bb3cb_text_export.jpeg)Alert: **You’ll get email notifications when your deposit is received, approved, or rejected.**8\. **Tap Deposit History to view all your mobile deposit statuses.**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-09/8f4dd25b-550a-4d5e-8746-1b754000c257/user_cropped_screenshot_4aaff53232964dbb815e9ed2b00ab46b_text_export.jpeg)9\. **Switch accounts at the top to view deposit history for different accounts. Status, amount, and reference number are shown for each deposit.**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-03/86ca3d72-83bf-4ff6-8cac-a657fe3dcdad/user_cropped_screenshot_48f2d1b06d6d44cd9aaf2fe44c801da4_text_export.jpeg)
