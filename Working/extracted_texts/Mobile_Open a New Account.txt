# Open a New Account in Mobile AppThis guide will help you open a new account using our mobile app, step by step. You will need your ID information and employment info.1\. **Tap "Apply for new account"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-03/308b52d3-ddf3-465a-8c48-53a3f6b12484/user_cropped_screenshot_7853cfaff80d42929b721dae3a076ccd_text_export.jpeg)2\. **Select the membership for the new account then hit "Next"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-03/92e2f37e-084f-4cf3-96b4-6ead5012d864/user_cropped_screenshot_1a36e6d92f8947ed9be777b4ca59393d_text_export.jpeg)3\. **Fill in the required information. Review the eConsent terms, mark the checkbox then tap "Save and Continue"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-09/7f81aade-20c2-4f91-a125-876897da165a/user_cropped_screenshot_6c26e343c0d642d6acb3f97ead233817_text_export.jpeg)Alert: **If you can’t continue, check that all required fields are filled in.**4\. **Review the address, then tap "Save and Continue"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-09/b862d8cb-3706-486b-896c-3277cbea9b04/user_cropped_screenshot_356f015b8afe4778b122499bb0af959e_text_export.jpeg)5\. **Enter in Employment History and add any additional Co-Applicants, then tap "Save and Continue"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-09/ced1da63-83a3-4fd5-aee9-840890b0fe3f/user_cropped_screenshot_a88ddbd8aa3b4159ad8c1e90968b24a1_text_export.jpeg)6\. **Select the accounts to open, then tap "Save and Continue"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-09/34084b86-79ad-441f-846a-511cf45d0a65/user_cropped_screenshot_df7d69fda2114c74befb689757d9ae70_text_export.jpeg)7\. **Select if you are subject to backup withholding, then tap "Save and continue"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-05-02/99ea6c78-8db1-4e88-91f7-4929bf61fc72/screenshot_0f5768f606af4b9c882b9cb283b41ca4_text_export.jpeg)8\. **Review the information and agree to the "General Inquiry Disclosure"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-05-02/8f26554d-af2e-46a5-b9d7-26f61b2f5d5d/user_cropped_screenshot_bd703f0978324f36979bbc6af2a147bc_text_export.jpeg)9\. **Review the Disclosures then check the box agreeing to the "Agreements and Disclosures", then select "Submit my application".**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-05-02/9d7ef049-f704-41df-9bd0-b6621b2db732/user_cropped_screenshot_99769c45433a4ef1b0eef24af72ec456_text_export.jpeg)Alert: **Someone will reach out to you with next steps after application review.**
