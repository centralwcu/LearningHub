# Sending Money to a Person on the Mobile AppThis feature can be used to send money to anyone, instantly. All you need is their email or phone number.Tip: When you use **Pay A Person**, the recipient will **receive a text or email** with steps to receive the funds. The options available to the recipient are to send the funds to their:- Account at Central Willamette (Instant transfer)- Account at another financial institution (1-2 business days)- Debit card (Instant transfer) (70 cent surcharge)1\.
