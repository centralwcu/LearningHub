# Make Internal Loan Payments on the Mobile AppThis guide demonstrates how to complete payments to internal CWCU loans on the Mobile App.1\. **Tap "Move Money"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-03/36fcf21f-abb1-4e22-917d-027d40aaa16f/user_cropped_screenshot_7d1ff831b46a4db5918e804ac764b0bb_text_export.jpeg)2\. **Tap "Internal Transfers and Payments"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-03/cd4faaf4-8598-4a73-a227-9a9bf1b8cf32/screenshot_c15657312b354563b24fcf60f641466e_text_export.jpeg)3\. **Tap "Select a source account" to select the account the payment will come out of**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-18/ef06e425-ce5b-4838-9989-47f2bbcbcb80/screenshot_004143caa0964f5c8ff9a63797771e2e_text_export.jpeg)4\. **Select an account**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-20/81ce435f-435b-4602-ba7f-a143fe44b405/user_cropped_screenshot_5d1bdbb55c5a48f3a05807ad1b5164a9_text_export.jpeg)5\. **Tap "Select destination account" to choose the loan you want to pay**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-20/94f50c5c-cfc7-406f-a65a-c06de07e8a9b/user_cropped_screenshot_8efc71f1e4d74087b0fa71c8b229331d_text_export.jpeg)6\. **When a loan is selected as the destination account, then the "Payment option" will appear and will let you select to make a regular payment, pay the next due amount, etc**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-20/32fb1594-1e33-4ef8-874a-ecb499b781ec/user_cropped_screenshot_6f9ee3e0c44c4549bc04b139bf184deb_text_export.jpeg)7\. **When you select "Make a regular payment" then you can enter an amount to pay in the "Enter amount" field**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-20/8620dedf-c90a-438a-9aea-16dd4216e0ca/user_cropped_screenshot_a3f8499f5f6c48c38dbbc18a74344ea6_text_export.jpeg)Tip: **The transfer date for the payment will default to today's date, but you can change the date to schedule a future payment and checking the box next to "Repeat transfer" will let you schedule recurring payments**8\. **After hitting "Transfer funds" you will be brought to the confirm screen. Review the information and hit "Confirm transfer"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-20/17dc868d-b356-405c-98d2-67ee3ac064b6/user_cropped_screenshot_b4be820bfe834b28a0cce95a2b0990ce_text_export.jpeg)9\. **Tap "Done"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-20/db8815fb-c7f0-49df-9faf-1108e5942010/user_cropped_screenshot_b859ab9f9a6846f6826cad636f62fa33_text_export.jpeg)
