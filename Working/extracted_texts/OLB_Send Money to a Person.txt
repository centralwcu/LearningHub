# Send Money to a PersonThis feature can be used to send money to anyone, instantly. All you need is their email or phone number.Tip: When you use **Pay A Person**, the recipient will **receive a text or email** with steps to receive the funds. The funds can be sent to their:- Account at Central Willamette (Instant transfer)- Account at another financial institution (1-2 business days)- Debit card (Instant transfer) (70 cent surcharge)1\. **Once logged in, click "Move
