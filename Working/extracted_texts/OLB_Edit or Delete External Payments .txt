# Edit or Delete External Payments OnlineIf you have set up a payment to another bank or person and need to cancel or remove it, follow these steps.1\. **Click "Move Money"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-09/0a5816e6-2d72-40bb-9e93-be16ed454c3d/user_cropped_screenshot_7d965afaa8dc4a2e85d78d18d87b7caf_text_export.jpeg)2\. **Click "External Transfers & Payments"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-09/f7a11ba6-b5e2-43c7-9ba1-c99e7e2bf578/screenshot_6af962c023374741bc076da526a77a69_text_export.jpeg)Tip: **Go to step 5 to delete a saved recipient!**3\. **Select the transaction to delete or edit**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-09/09007e64-6b2b-4bc1-84ab-d41521eec093/user_cropped_screenshot_a598d84ddd7d4fa6ba8d6f4cb72d915b_text_export.jpeg)4\. **Scroll down and select delete or edit**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-09/7e23be74-da4d-401c-ace0-85a9ef5745e5/user_cropped_screenshot_7b62be23b17f4205a1ebee67748d0977_text_export.jpeg)5\. **Click "Pay A Person"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-05-12/fed17726-7445-4cd5-bc5b-fac4b19c1217/ascreenshot_91298a65409b4b198f90b46f6c00072d_text_export.jpeg)6\. **The pencil icon looks like a small drawing pencil. This means you can edit the information for that person. Click it to see more options.**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-09/cdd34a5b-4d38-4a50-aa9e-2585344229f9/user_cropped_screenshot_d3a98cc227dd41ebbf1f3c129b690260_text_export.jpeg)7\. **The trash can icon means 'delete.' Click this to remove the person from your list.**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-09/83c81696-0a4a-4f9a-918a-becdfb0edf2f/user_cropped_screenshot_72b8ebbc61f84b8da270f91ce5dc719f_text_export.jpeg)8\. **Click "Delete"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-09/7b28c727-db94-415d-9ca0-289fab8bef29/user_cropped_screenshot_f12764f794e844e691d88c9745fbcfe0_text_export.jpeg)Tip: **If you have trouble deleting a payment or recipient, please contact the support team for help at the number below in the footer, or by submitting a ticket on the 'Support' page.**
