# Add an External Account for External TransfersThis guide is designed to show you how to add your accounts from another financial institution for external transfers.1\. **Tap "Move Money"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-03/516d2e3f-270e-41e2-a168-9f3a5d1eabb3/user_cropped_screenshot_be6eb3ba61ae4c1c801114fc0a288d6e_text_export.jpeg)2\. **Tap "External Transfers and Payments"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-03/cf4cbec2-a450-4416-a0de-73bde8f87c4b/screenshot_f0fc1e2bca164e60bb20411824152bd9_text_export.jpeg)3\. **Tap "Transfer"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-03/7a0a1e05-d94d-4265-9763-2f0327914052/screenshot_3801e740c6c145e3984d63d7b32fa66f_text_export.jpeg)4\. **Select the "To" box and then "ADD EXTERNAL ACCOUNT"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-04-25/b6c03cc2-8a6c-4f69-a520-93daa90baa4b/user_cropped_screenshot_b17ba3d8eeb54fa593bf8cd7d5523cef_text_export.jpeg)5\. **Search for the institution's name. Popular institutions are automatically shown**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-11-26/4b794810-2a66-4e1d-9af0-84ead22fb7f3/user_cropped_screenshot_4dea40ae6f1d4f7c846ac5d5c0b45ab0_text_export.jpeg)6\. **You will be prompted to login to your institution to verify.**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-11-26/29f31d40-03ec-45f6-b722-b9362cf41969/user_cropped_screenshot_46fd88b8dd3b4c01aec56a54ba14c19e_text_export.jpeg)Tip: **Your newly added External Account will be available to use immediately if you used this method and logged into your other financial institution**7\. **If you cannot find your institution, then you can enter in the account details manually**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-11-27/f170243c-fe95-4c9d-b2ab-1ae90cbc65c2/user_cropped_screenshot_2ae98fb322314e05a2c1da919aabc745_text_export.jpeg)8\. **Fill out the required information**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-11-27/19ee6fa6-0032-4b77-89ed-2e73fe5a4d49/user_cropped_screenshot_e3576dc0416f4c348f3648d8b0b743f0_text_export.jpeg)9\. **Tap 'Continue'**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-11-27/a807b248-52bb-4c8a-9320-15804d345cb8/user_cropped_screenshot_022c6e9e058d46aa89c6afb4f96b0b3c_text_export.jpeg)10\. **A confirmation message will pop up.**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-11-27/17855146-5931-4479-9085-31f3cc91392a/user_cropped_screenshot_dadc4f02f4f44462a1dd06b88f15f616_text_export.jpeg)11\. **A code will be inside the transaction details once the transaction arrives at your other Financial Institution. This may take one to three business days and it is a three or four-digit code and may appear differently than below depending on your Financial Institution.**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-11-27/700fc93e-765a-463f-9a07-a6d23fbee7ec/screenshot_ac0dbdd61b1b47bf91db5fdb7f14aa7f_text_export.jpeg)12\. **You will need to verify this code after it is received. To do this, Tap on "External Transfers & Payments and tap on the three vertical dots next to the question mark while on the "Activity" page**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-03/e2a72ed3-ea7c-451e-90d6-ed3c1703cd07/screenshot_84694e7432674a4896ae7c79e3d7b0f5_text_export.jpeg)13\. **Tap on "Transfer"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-03/24016a25-c87e-40bd-be73-d89b19af4a34/screenshot_97f0bcdb86f344d3bb42267ac72e66bd_text_export.jpeg)14\. **Tap on "Manage Accounts"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-03/795ca0e8-c4b8-4b01-a1b7-402c979c00f1/user_cropped_screenshot_ec0ba72d6a31475a801fac5f92d4700b_text_export.jpeg)15\. **At the bottom of this page, you will see the External Account that was added is 'Pending'. Tap "Verify"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-03/7dffae04-fb96-4647-a4c7-60253bd7f7e2/screenshot_58eae20e59b84441aa7447c2f10c9284_text_export.jpeg)16\. **This page gives you an example of the code. Tap "Next"**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-09/232cec79-8661-4088-a366-4624b3b6a500/user_cropped_screenshot_de9ba60fbce0470d82ce6c874a7f436c_text_export.jpeg)17\. **Enter your code and hit "Continue" to finish**![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-03/dcd3ce24-ae2c-45da-b70d-1e2dc559cc5f/screenshot_54617361a3014eeb9a400185b954d8cc_text_export.jpeg)
