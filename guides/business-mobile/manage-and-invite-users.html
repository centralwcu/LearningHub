Add and Manage Business Users

This guide shows how to quickly add, configure, and manage users in your business account.

1
Tap on the menu icon
2
Tap "Business Management"
3
Tap "User Management"
4
Tap "Add a new user"
5
Enter in the user's name, email address and phone number
6
Assign a role to the user then select the accounts the user should have access to then tap "Add User"
7
The user will receive an invite via email and will need to select "Join Business"
8
The user can login with credentials if they already have a login with Central Willamette or can complete the enrollment process
9
To change the users you are viewing on the User Management page tap on "Active Users"
10
Users can be filtered by Active, Invited, Locked and Deactivated
11
Tap on any user to edit their information
12
Tap the "x" next to a business account to remove the user's access. You can also reset their password or deactivate their user profile
