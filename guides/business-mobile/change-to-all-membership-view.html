Change to All Membership View

The 'All Membership View' is an easier way to see all of your accounts together, Business and Personal. When in this view, Business and Personal accounts will show together on the Accounts Page and also together in the list when making an Internal Transfer.

1
Tap on the menu icon
2
Tap "Switch profile"
3
Tap "All Memberships"
ⓘ
Tapping on the bookmark icon next to a view will make that view the default view that opens whenever logging in
4
The All Membership view displays personal and business accounts together on the Accounts page and also when completing Internal transfers
