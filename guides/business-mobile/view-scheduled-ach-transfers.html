View Scheduled ACH Transfers

This guide walks you through the steps for: View Scheduled ACH Transfers.

1
Tap on the menu icon
2
Tap "Business Management"
3
Tap "Scheduled External Transfers"
4
Tap on "Member Number"
5
Select which account you want to view
6
Tap "Select"
7
Select the method used to initiate the ACH transfer
8
Select "Edit" to adjust a scheduled transfer's amount
9
The transfer amount is the only thing that can be edited. If anything else needs to be changed then delete the transfer and initiate a new one
10
Tap on "Delete" to cancel a scheduled transfer
11
Tap "OK" to confirm the deletion
