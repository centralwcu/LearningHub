Send an ACH Transfer

This guide explains how to submit an ACH transfer for payment or collection in the mobile banking app.

1
Tap on the menu icon
2
Tap on "Business Management"
3
Tap on "ACH Transfer"
4
Another way to send an ACH transfer is by going to the Move Money page and tapping on "ACH Transfer"
5
Select whether the ACH Transfer is for sending or receiving money
6
Select the Source account that the funds will come out of
7
Select the "To" account that the funds will go into
8
Enter in the amount of the transfer
9
Select the SEC code
ⓘ
Select PPD when the recipient is an individual and the payment is being sent to their personal checking or savings account.

Select CCD when the recipient is a business, company, or other organization and the payment is being sent to its business bank account.
10
Entering a memo is optional
11
Select the Company Entry Description
ⓘ
Payment: Select this option for a general ACH payment. The recipient may see “Payment” as the description of the transaction in their account history.

Custom: Select this option to create a short description for the ACH payment, such as “Invoice 1234,” “Rent,” or “Vendor Pay.” Use a clear description that will help the recipient recognize the transaction.
12
Select the Effective Date, if the transfer is recurring, and then "Transfer Funds"
Transfers submitted before 4:00 PM PST will be completed by the Effective Date, if the transfer is submitted after 4:00 PM PST then it will be completed the business day after the Effective Date
13
Review the transfer information then select "Confirm Transfer"
14
A One-Time Passcode will be required
15
The ACH Transfer has been submitted successfully
