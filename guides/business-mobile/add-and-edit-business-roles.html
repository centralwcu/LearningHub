Create and Manage Business Banking Roles

This guide shows how to create and modify business roles so you can control who can access which features and set transaction limits for ACH transfers. It walks through adding a role, assigning feature permissions, configuring one or multiple limits, and editing role details.

1
Tap the menu icon
2
Tap "Business Management"
3
Tap "Role Management"
4
Tap on "Add"
5
Enter the role name, description, select a template then tap "Next"
6
Select the features the role should have access to
7
Tap "Save"
8
Tap "Add Limit" if the new role has access to ACH payments
9
Tap "Add Limit" again
10
Select which limit to add
11
Enter in an amount for the selected limit
12
Tapping "Add Another Limit" allows more limits to be set
13
Tap "Save" after setting the limits
14
To edit a role, tap on any role from the Role Management page and select from the options that appear at the bottom of the page
15
The Role Settings is where you can edit the role name, description or deactivate the role
