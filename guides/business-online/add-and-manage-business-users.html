Add and Manage Business Users

Learn the step-by-step process for inviting a new user to your business account and managing existing users. This guide simplifies the user management portal, ensuring you can quickly invite team members and configure their access permissions. These users do not need to be a signer on your business and will only have access to the accounts they are invited to.

1
Click "Business Banking"
2
Click "User Management"
3
Click "Add a new user" to invite a user to have access to the business account
4
Enter in the user's name, email address and mobile number
5
Open the "Assign role" dropdown menu
6
Select a role
7
Select the accounts to give the user access to
8
Click "Add user"
9
If the user is invited successfully a green box will appear at the top
10
The user will receive an invite via email to join the business
11
The invitation link will bring the user to this page. If they already have login credentials with Central Willamette, then they can select "Yes" and log in with those credentials to accept the invitation. If they do not have login credentials, then they will select "No".
12
They then click "Submit" and follow the steps to finish enrollment
13
To manage Users, go to the "User management" page
14
Filter the users with these tabs
15
Click on a user to edit their information or permissions
16
Use the top options to edit the user, reset their password or deactivate the user
17
Click the "x" next to an account to remove the user from the business
18
Click "Yes" to remove the user from the business
