Manage Business Banking Recipients

Learn how to efficiently edit or remove recipients from your business banking account in Online Banking.

1
Click "Business Banking"
2
Click "Recipient Management"
3
Select the recipient to be edited or deleted
4
Click "Edit recipient" to change the recipient's name
5
Click the three dots next to "Transfer Funds" then click "More details" to view the recipient's saved account information
6
Clicking the eyeball will reveal the recipient's saved account number
7
Click "Add Account" to add a new account to the recipient
8
Click "Remove recipient" to delete the recipient
9
A green confirmation message will appear when the recipient has been removed successfully
